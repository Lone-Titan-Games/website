Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Coil, a mobile game by Lone Titan Games, a sole proprietorship based in Ontario, Canada ("we", "us"). Questions: support@lonetitangames.com.

Your save

Coil has no accounts and no servers of ours, and never asks for your name or email. Your progress, coins, gems, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them from the device.

If your device's backup is turned on, Android may automatically back up the game's save to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall Coil. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Backup, or Settings → System → Backup).

Crash reports and play statistics (Google Firebase)

To fix bugs and to learn which parts of the game work, Coil uses two Google Firebase services. Google runs them for us. They are the only information about the game we receive, and we see it only as reports.

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a report: what went wrong and where in the game's code, the device model, operating system and app version, the device's state at that moment (such as free memory), and a random ID for this installation of the game. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). These record how the game is played: when it is opened and for how long, levels started and finished (how long they took, hearts lost and power-ups picked), the rewarded ads offered and watched, Workshop upgrades and in-game purchases. With them go a random ID for this installation of the game, the device model, operating system, language and app version, and your country and city, worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics does not keep the IP address itself. The detailed records are kept for 2 months; after that, only totals remain (for example, how many players came back the next day).

Neither service learns your name, email or Google account. The play statistics don't use your advertising ID and are not used to show you ads. We don't let Google use this data for its own products. How Google handles it: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, no play statistics are sent until you have answered the game's privacy message. If you don't agree, Google Analytics keeps no ID for you. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. Crash reports are sent for every player, because we need them to keep the game working.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Coil shows ads through Google AdMob, including ads you choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads, and crash and performance diagnostics of the ad software.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Where you have agreed to it, Google may use it to show you personalized ads. How Google uses it: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown and can change their choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the game. On Android, anyone can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in the device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying No Ads in the game removes the ads shown between levels.

Purchases

In-game purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or billing details. We receive only a confirmation of what was bought, so the game can deliver it. Google Play keeps a record of your purchases on your Google account: permanent purchases such as No Ads can be brought back on a reinstall or a new device with Restore Purchases in the game's Store. If something you bought is missing, write to us with your Google Play order number and we will help.

Children

Coil is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and want information about them removed from Google's advertising systems, contact us and we will help.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, and we share none with anyone other than Google, for the services described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, and to object to its use. We hold no names, emails or accounts. The crash reports and play statistics carry only random IDs and are deleted after the times given above. Requests about advertising data are best made to Google through the links above. You can also write to us, and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
